# Reference: Property Discovery & Intelligence

## Purpose

Design decisions without business truth produce empty decoration. Before drawing wireframes, choosing palettes, or typing a line of CSS, the agent must uncover the authentic story, constraints, and operational realities of the hospitality property.

---

## 1. Primary Research Vectors

Investigate the property across multiple public angles:

### A. Digital Footprint & Official Channels
- **Official Website (if one exists)**:
  - Architecture, current messaging, booking engine or inquiry flow.
  - Room categories, stated amenities, rate structures.
  - Visual identity: logos, existing typography, legacy photography.
- **Social Channels (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok)**:
  - Tone of voice in social captions and community interactions.
  - Unfiltered guest tags and tagged stories (crucial for unvarnished reality).
  - High-engagement reels, behind-the-scenes video, drone footage.
  - Operating announcements, seasonal closures, promotional packages.

### B. Guest Sentiment & Verbatim Realities
- **Google Maps & Local Listings**:
  - Review volume, aggregate star rating, response rate from management.
  - Recurring praise: What specific room, view, food item, or staff member do guests consistently highlight?
  - Recurring friction: Unpaved roads, power cuts, dampness, cold nights, noisy neighbors, confusing check-in.
  - Plus Codes, exact geographic coordinates, terrain context.
- **OTA Portals (Booking.com, Agoda, Airbnb, TripAdvisor)**:
  - Exact bed types, square footage, bathroom types, bathroom fixtures.
  - Policies: cancellation, children, pet policies, check-in windows.

### C. Competitive & Regional Context
- **Cluster & Neighbors**: Who operates within a 10 km radius?
- **Differentiation Factor**: Why does a guest pick this property over neighboring options?
- **Regional Geography**: What is the access route? What weather patterns dominate (monsoon, fog, snow, arid heat)?

---

## 2. Evidence Tagging System

To maintain absolute factual integrity, all claims in research reports must use explicit evidence tags:

| Tag | Meaning | Requirement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `[VERIFIED]` | Confirmed by official statement, verified signage, or clear management policy. | Cite direct URL or document. |
| `[THIRD-PARTY]` | Reported by guests, travel bloggers, or OTA listings, but not officially stated. | Corroborate across ≥2 sources. |
| `[UNCONFIRMED]` | Plausible claim with single-source attribution; needs client verification. | Flag in client onboarding. |
| `[CONFLICT]` | Contradictory information across platforms (e.g., pricing or room counts). | Highlight discrepancy explicitly. |

## 3. Core Intelligence Extraction Framework

For every property, extract answers to these nine foundational questions:

1. **What is the core physical proposition?** (e.g. stilted bamboo huts perched above cloud sea; heritage colonial villa with tea plantation vistas; minimalist cliffside concrete pavilion).
2. **Who is the actual visitor?** (e.g. urban couples seeking quiet retreat; budget adventure trekkers; generational family reunions; remote creative workers).
3. **What is the journey friction?** (e.g. 6-hour mountain truck ride; ferry crossing; remote dirt track requiring 4WD). *Note: High friction often demands romanticizing the journey, not hiding it.*
4. **What are the signature moments?** (e.g. dawn mist rolling across the private balcony deck; communal dinner cooked over firewood; night stargazing without light pollution).
5. **What is the food & dining situation?** (e.g. fixed local indigenous buffet; à la carte chef menu; bring-your-own rations; complimentary breakfast only).
6. **What is the booking workflow?** (e.g. direct phone call / WhatsApp messaging; automated credit card gateway; bank transfer with advance deposit slip).
7. **What is the price reality?** (e.g. fixed weekday vs weekend surges, seasonal monsoon discounts, meal inclusion rules).
8. **What are the property's honest limitations?** (e.g. no air conditioning due to off-grid solar; shared bathrooms; limited Wi-Fi; steep stairs unsuitable for mobility-impaired guests).
9. **What is the cultural or linguistic vernacular?** (e.g. indigenous language naming conventions, architectural traditions, local materials like shitol pati mats or terracotta tiles).
